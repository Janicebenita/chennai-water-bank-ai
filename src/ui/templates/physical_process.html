FROM RAINFALL TO DECISION TO PHYSICAL ACTION

SENSE → UNDERSTAND → DECIDE → ACT → VERIFY

Chennai Water Bank connects physical water infrastructure with sensing, software intelligence and controlled mechanical routing. Sensors describe what is happening, software determines the appropriate response, controllers translate that decision into actuator commands, and feedback sensors verify the physical result.

SIMULATED SENSOR VALUES: generated from the current simulation/model. A deployed system would receive values from certified physical or industrial sensors.SIMULATED CONTROL: a deployed command would travel through an edge controller or PLC, motor driver or relay, and physical actuator.MODELLED RECHARGE WARNING: requires certified, site-specific testing, treatment design, hydrogeological review and regulatory approval.
